Agency-facing form

Please provide the calendar year 2026 medical insurance premium amounts for your agency under the State Group Insurance Program. Include the months your agency participated in 2026, even if it participated for only part of the year.

Use totals from your payroll or financial records. Enter annual dollar amounts, not monthly amounts or percentages. Include the medical premiums for covered dependents where applicable. Exclude dental, vision, and post-65 retiree coverage, including The Tennessee Plan.

Export and staff QA

  1. Export the nine fields above along with Formstack submission ID and submitted date. Retain each original submission; keep staff corrections and follow-up notes separately with a trace back to the original.
  2. Match each submission against Joshua’s contact roster of agencies that participated at any time in 2026, including those that terminated during 2026. For combined reports, match every listed agency or location and count the three amounts only once.
  3. Calculate total active reported = agency_active_paid + employee_active_paid. Compare this with Edison active premium collections for the same agency or combined group and same calendar-year participation period. Compare pre-65 retiree amount with the applicable retiree data as a review aid, recognizing that the employer amount is a share of the total.
  4. Compare each figure and its employer/employee share with the prior year and investigate outliers, zeros, unexpected duplicate coverage, or one figure containing the other. Flag for review rather than rejecting the agency’s submission.
  5. After follow-up, reconcile the final dataset and send aggregate totals to Jennifer Lewis for the downstream calculations. Keep the initial, reminder, and overdue follow-up emails as a separate outreach process.

Examples to test: a standard local education response; a local government response combining two agencies; all Tennessee State Veterans Home locations reported together; a newly enrolled or terminated agency with only part-year amounts; legitimate zeros; a blank combined-agency list; and a mistyped large amount.
