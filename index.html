Annual enrollment, one step at a time.

Follow the screens in Edison, choose your benefits, and confirm that your enrollment was submitted. Select your employer group below to tailor the steps.

Adapted from the supplied “Higher Ed, Local Ed & Local Gov Employee Self Service (ESS) Instructions.” Edison is the source for your available plans and current enrollment period.

Your route through Edison

0 of 7 steps marked complete

    Step 1 · Get started

    Sign in to Edison

    Start at www.edison.tn.gov. Use the path that matches the button you choose on the sign-in page.

    Green “Benefits Enrollment” button

    Select Benefits Enrollment, then Login. Once signed in, select the Annual Enrollment tile.

    Red “Employee Portal Login” button

    Sign in, then go to Benefits & Health → Benefits Enrollment.

    1. On the Benefits Enrollment page, select Start.
    2. Read the welcome page, then select Next in the upper right corner.

    Using a phone?

    These instructions describe a computer screen. Buttons may appear in different places on a mobile device.

    Step 2 · Review your information

    Check your address and phone number

    Edison shows these on separate screens. Review each before continuing.

    1. Address: Select the arrow (>) next to an address to change it, or the plus sign (+) to add one. Select Save if you make a change, then Next.
    2. Phone number: Select the arrow (>) to make a change. Select Save if you make a change, then Next.
    3. Consumer-driven health plan page: If you are not enrolling in a consumer-driven health plan, you can select Next without making a change.

    Save each edit

    Selecting Next is separate from selecting Save. Save your address or phone change before leaving that screen.

    Step 3 · Dependents

    Add dependents and documents, if needed

    The Dependent Information page lets you add a new dependent. If you are not adding one, continue to the next screen.

    I need to add a dependent
    1. Select Add Dependent. Enter the requested information; fields marked with an asterisk are required. Scroll to see all fields, then select Save in the upper right corner.
    2. When Edison says supporting documents are required, select OK.
    3. Select Upload Documents in the Attachment column. Edison shows which documents are needed for the dependent type you added.
    4. Choose the document Category, then select Add Attachment → My Device. Find the file, select Upload, then Done.
    5. Repeat for any additional attachments. When finished, select Done. If Edison says approval is required, select OK.

    Documents still to upload?

    The source instructions say you can return and upload them before the end of the enrollment period. Check the current deadline in Edison.

    I need another way to send supporting documents

    The source lists fax 615-741-8196 or Benefits Support (Zendesk). If you do not upload directly in Edison, put your name and employee ID on every page. The source says the employee ID is on the front of your CVS Caremark card.

    Once you have finished with any new dependents, select Next. You can also add or update a dependent while reviewing a plan later.

    Step 4 · Higher education only

    Review beneficiaries

    Higher education employees see a beneficiary review page after Dependent Information.

    1. Select each tab and review the information shown.
    2. Make any needed updates, verify the information, then select Next.

    Local education and local government employees do not see this page; the guide will skip this step for them.

    Step 5 · Plan choices

    Review every benefit tile

    The Annual Enrollment page is your summary screen. It shows monthly insurance costs and the benefit tiles available to you.

    Local education and local government costs

    Your final cost can differ from the monthly amount shown because it depends on how much your agency contributes toward the premium.

    1. Scroll to Benefit Plans and select the Medical tile to review medical choices.
    2. If you want a dependent covered on the medical plan, select the checkbox next to that person’s name. You can use Add/Update Dependent if needed.
    3. When you choose a medical plan, select Select, then Done. If you make no changes, select Cancel.
    4. Open each other available benefit tile and review the options and information carefully. You only see tiles for plans for which you are eligible.

    What do “Changed” and “Visited” mean here?

    The tile status updates as you work through your benefit choices. Review every available tile before submission.

    Step 6 · Submit

    Submit and read the messages

    After reviewing your choices, select Submit Enrollment in the upper right corner of the Annual Enrollment page. Its location may differ on a phone.

    The pop-up is part of submitting

    Read its messages and select Done or View in the pop-up. The source instructions say this action is required for your benefit choices to be submitted.

    Red circle: error

    Read the message and fix the issue. Errors must be cleared to submit successfully.

    Yellow triangle: warning

    A warning may mean another action is needed, such as dependent documents or a statement of health for disability enrollment. A warning may still allow submission. If you already sent the documents, the source says no further action is needed for that document warning.

    Step 7 · Verify

    Make sure it says “Submitted”

    Use these checks before you consider your enrollment complete.

    • The Status on the Annual Enrollment page says Submitted.
    • You can select View to see your Enrollment Preview statement.
    • You can use Expand All or open each section to review the statement, then Print View to save or print it.

    No preview statement?

    Go back to the Annual Enrollment page and select Submit Enrollment. Review and resolve any messages, then check the status again.

    Preview versus final confirmation

    The preview is not your final enrollment statement. After the enrollment period ends, the source says an email will let you know when you can log in to Edison to view or print the final confirmation statement. If your current email address is in Edison, you should also receive an email confirming receipt of your enrollment.

    If you return to annual enrollment, Edison takes you to the last page you worked on. Use Benefits Statements to review your preview. To make a change during the enrollment period, select Annual Enrollment on the left and repeat the process. Submit again and verify the status afterward.

    This is a navigation aid based on the supplied ESS PDF. Use Edison and current Partners for Health materials for deadlines, eligibility, plan costs, required documents and final enrollment records. Progress in this guide is saved only in this browser; it does not submit information to Edison.
